SØRPEDIA — DESIGN DIRECTION

Identity



Nordic Industrial Intelligence.



Sørpedia combines Scandinavian industrial retail, product intelligence, engineering configuration and warehouse operations.



It must NOT resemble:



A traditional hardware store.

A generic Bootstrap dashboard.

A generic AI-generated SaaS template.

A gaming interface.

A crypto trading dashboard.

Visual direction



Combine:



Brutalist Scandinavian architecture.

Precision engineering software.

Premium outdoor equipment retail.

Industrial logistics control rooms.

Refined SaaS dashboard components.

Design system

Deep pine-charcoal backgrounds.

Warm birch and muted amber accents.

Sør-Tre red reserved for important actions and status.

Editorial display typography.

Technical monospace labels.

Large imagery and asymmetric layouts.

Thin borders, restrained glass effects.

Generous negative space.

High-quality responsive behavior.

Three distinct interface experiences

CONTROL TOWER



An industrial operations command center.



Isometric warehouse visualization.

Incoming and sorting product flows.

Interactive operational stations.

Live analytics event stream.

KPI visualizations.

Search gaps and customer demand intelligence.

BUILDLAB



An engineering configuration workspace.



Large central assembly area.

Product selection and specification panels.

Combined price, weight and relevant technical calculations.

Compatibility relationships.

Missing component recommendations.

Clear verified/inferred/unknown states.

PRODUCT INTELLIGENCE



A product knowledge environment.



Existing product catalogue.

Existing deterministic product guide.

Product relationships and provenance.

Søren with contextual product information.

Customer search and recommendation insights.

Critical requirements

Audit existing functionality before modifying anything.

Preserve the existing deterministic product guide.

Reuse existing product data, APIs, analytics events and relationships.

Do not invent product specifications, inventory or compatibility.

Distinguish real prototype events from simulated warehouse activity.

Preserve existing Søren animations exactly as supplied.

No generic card-heavy layouts.

No unnecessary refactoring.

Responsive desktop and mobile layouts.

Accessibility and reduced-motion support.

Implementation approach



First inspect the repository and identify existing components, routes, data structures and APIs.



Then propose a concise implementation plan.



Implement in independent, testable stages. Preserve existing functionality and verify each integration.



Prioritize a complete, polished customer journey over unfinished features.
