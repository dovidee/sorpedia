# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Customers of a Norwegian specialist hardware retailer shopping online: tradespeople who know what they need and want facts fast, homeowners and cabin owners who need guidance to pick the right item and the accessories that go with it, and store staff who look up product facts for customers. Typical context is a phone or laptop at home or on a job, often comparing two or three similar items before driving to a store.

## Product Purpose

Sørpedia gives online customers the same guidance and confidence as a visit to the store. It shows every specification with its source and level, what goes well with a product and why, reviews from verified buyers, stock per store, and a deterministic product guide. Success is measured as fewer zero result searches, more orders with a matching accessory, more specifications per product and fewer escalated chats.

## Brand Personality

Precise, sturdy, knowledgeable. The voice is plain Norwegian bokmål from an experienced staff member: concrete, short, no marketing language. The interface should feel engineered and trustworthy, like a well run trade counter rather than a campaign site.

## Anti-references

A traditional hardware store flyer, a generic Bootstrap or SaaS dashboard template, an AI generated landing page with cards and gradients, gaming or crypto interfaces, luxury retail with decorative serif type. No decorative labels, breadcrumbs or numbering that carry no information, and no repeated generic copy on every page.

## Design Principles

1. Facts first: product identity, price, stock and specifications are always visible and always from the database.
2. Show the source: every value carries its provenance; unknown is a valid answer and assumed is never shown as certain.
3. One clear action per view: red is reserved for the action that moves the customer forward and for status.
4. Structure over decoration: hierarchy comes from type, grid and space, not from boxes, badges or effects.
5. Each page answers its own question: home introduces, category pages explain how to choose, product pages help evaluate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Visible keyboard focus on every control, semantic HTML, native form controls kept under custom styling, reduced motion respected, light and dark mode following the device unless the user picks one in Settings. Norwegian first with an English toggle.
