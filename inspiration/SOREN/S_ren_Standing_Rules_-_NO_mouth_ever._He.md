# Søren — Standing Rules

- NO mouth, ever. He never talks. No mouth line, smile, grimace or teeth.
- Expressions come ONLY from his eyes, used exactly as drawn: solid black oval eyes with small white highlight dots (closed arc eyes where the pose sheet has them).
- NEVER give him white eyeballs with human-style pupils (too creepy).
- Appearance otherwise unchanged: white/cream trunk, red leaf canopy, red boots. No filters or recolouring.
- - All animations loop seamlessly.
- Eyes never switch state mid-clip (closed happy arcs stay closed; black dot eyes stay black dots).
- Motion must be clearly active (wave = real side-to-side wave; idle = leaves visibly blowing in wind). Shrug = one slow shrug, then back to normal pose — not constant.
- Natural variation within the pose is OK (e.g. glancing back while pushing the cart, glancing toward the viewer), as long as he stays in that pose (shrug stays shrugging).
