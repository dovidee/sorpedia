# 022 Control Tower

Staff operations floor at `/ansatt/tarn`. One full-screen panel that replaces the old staff pages: the merge queue, the metrics and customer service all live here. The old merge queue, metrics and customer service pages are removed; their POST actions stay and redirect back to the tower. The full delivery contract is `inspiration/CONTROLTOWER/DELIVERY.md`, section 5. Build order follows `inspiration/CONTROLTOWER/PRIORITY.md`.

## Scope

In scope (PRIORITY phases 1 to 5, then DELIVERY 5.9 and 5.10):

1. Isometric floor drawn with plain Three.js (vendored in `public/assets/vendor/`, no CDN, no build step). Stations are named for what lands there and carry an icon: Alle hendelser (intake), Søk med treff, Søk uten treff, Guider, Spørsmål til Søren, Til kundeservice, Data å rette, Kjøp, Ukjent hendelse and Varemottak (the goods belt). "Forklaring" lists one line per station.
2. Event parcels: one per `analytics_events` row of today (Europe/Oslo). The type decides the station (`Tower::ROUTES`). Unknown types go to Ukjent.
3. Product parcels: products with an active revision, an open error report, an open ticket, or a place on an inbound shipment that is still on the way. They rest at Kvalitet (revisions or reports), else Kundeservice (ticket), else Mottak.
4. Live: new rows are polled every 5 seconds and travel along the belt to their station. Nothing moves without a new row. A full reload runs every 5 minutes.
5. Inspector beside the floor (never a modal). Event: ID, type, stations, time (Europe/Oslo), permitted payload fields. Product: name, ID and price from the database, specifications with source and version, missing facet keys, revisions with Flett and Avvis, error reports with Fjern verdi and Riktig (existing routes), stock marked DEMO. Shift-click selects several parcels and shows counts per station and type.
6. Floor column "I dag": six figure tiles (searches, rate without results, questions to Søren, rate passed to a person, guides, purchases), specifications by source as a segmented bar, data quality as meters, and a link to Innsikt.
7. Congestion: Kvalitet and Kundeservice turn red when their open items pass `Tower::CONGESTED`.
8. Fallbacks: without WebGL the floor becomes a table per station. With reduced motion, parcels are placed without travel.

9. Header: icon and text views Gulv, Transport, Kundeservice (with the number of open cases) and Innsikt, and "Spør Søren". No period, station or type filters. On open the floor plays the latest 40 rows of the day along the belts in time order, then goes live; polling waits until it ends. Station counts are also written as text outside the canvas. Under the floor, Lagerflyt shows 14 days per day: units in (arrived inbound shipments and refunds) above the line, units sold below, the net change as a line, and units still on the way as a dashed column (`Insight::stockFlow`).
10. Transport view (DELIVERY 5.9): SVG map from local Natural Earth 1:10m data (`public/assets/tower/norway.json`: land, neighbours, county borders, lakes, main roads, towns; public domain), no tile service. Each vehicle is labelled with its fleet number and origin to destination; the selected route shows its start and end. Markers sit at the latest stored position and never move between rows. Routes are dashed and labelled estimated. Positions older than 15 minutes are shown as stale. Shipment inspector: origin to destination, progress estimated from the route, timeline in Europe/Oslo with variance, vehicle, driver, cargo with links to the product inspector, weights (declared, measured, item sum, remaining payload as calculated, mismatch flagged), position history. Registration and phone only for `support` and `admin`. A field without a value is left out of the inspector, never printed as unknown; demo shipments are seeded with container, line weights, declared total and ETA. The cargo is a photo grid (picture, quantity, weight), and the product inspector shows the product photo. All fixtures come from `scripts/transport-demo.php` (also a step of `scripts/import.php`) and are marked DEMO DATA.
11. Tickets (DELIVERY 5.10): a new ticket needs a product. Søren asks the customer to confirm the product on the page, or to pick one with the search suggestions; Søren never searches on its own. The server checks the product exists. With nothing selected the inspector lists open tickets (also those without a product); a ticket opens with customer alias, product, the chat log, messages (live over SSE), reply and close (the existing customer service routes). Error reports get "Vurdering fra Søren", an assessment for staff only; nothing is sent or changed. "Utkast fra Søren" puts a draft from Ollama in the reply box; nothing is sent until staff press Send. If Ollama does not answer, no draft is made up.
12. Event actions (`EventDesk`): the inspector for one event shows its details and four actions. Related searches lists up to 20 other searches with the same term, with time and hit count. Mark as reviewed stores who and when in `event_reviews`; the event row itself is never changed. Product mapping (roles `ansatt` and `admin`) adds `produkt:ID` to the term in `search_synonyms`, so the next search for the term shows that product first; an existing synonym keeps its expansions. Improvement task stores a title and an optional product in `improvement_tasks`; open tasks are listed in the empty inspector and can be closed.
13. Demo delivery (`DemoDelivery`): "Demolevering til meg" in the transport view asks the browser for its position (or, if refused, takes a click on the map), and the server starts `SR-DEMO-LIVE`, a demo van from the nearest town at least 20 km away. It arrives in 180 seconds of real time; the speed-up is shown in the ETA source. The browser asks for a tick every second and each tick stores one `shipment_positions` row, so the marker moves only on stored rows. The destination is rounded to 3 decimals of a degree. The map shows the recipient pin and an arrival countdown. The van carries the lines of the latest customer order not refilled before; on arrival those quantities are added back to that store's online stock in the same transaction as the status change, and the refill is logged as a `stock`/`restock` event so an order is refilled only once. "Avslutt demo" deletes the demo shipment; stock changes stay.

14. Simulated GPS (`DemoGps`, DEMO DATA): while the page is open it asks `POST /api/tarn/gps/tick` every 8 seconds. For each demo shipment on the way the server stores one `shipment_positions` row (source `demo-gps`, at most one per 8 seconds) at the share of the stored route given by the time between departure and ETA. A loading demo vehicle departs at its planned time; one that reaches its ETA is delivered and its units go into stock. The inspector shows GPS source, speed and direction from the last two rows. "Min posisjon" shows the browser position on the map only; it is never sent.
15. Kundeservice view: open or all tickets (waiting for an answer marked), the conversation with Søren and the thread (live over SSE), reply, "Utkast fra Søren", close; the customer's orders with product photos; this week's searches without results with "Lag oppgave" (catalogue staff).
16. Refunds (`Refund`, roles `support`, `ansatt`, `admin`): from a ticket, staff refund chosen units or the whole order of that ticket's customer, after a confirm step that names the amount. Each line is a row in `order_refunds`; units go back to the online stock of the order's warehouse; the order becomes `refundert` when every unit is refunded; the customer gets a message in the ticket that says no money moved (demo payment). More units than are left, products not on the order and orders of another customer are refused (409).
17. Innsikt view (`Insight::build`): the last 7 days against the 7 before as tiles with change and sparklines; searches per day for 30 days and orders per day for 60 days with a linear trend, a 7 day forecast and a 95 % band, R² and the mean absolute error on the last 7 days held out (`Stats::forecast`; withheld below 14 days of data); search to cart to purchase; order values as a histogram with mean, median and the middle half; specifications by source as a donut; relations by level; data quality meters; search terms without results; days of stock left (online stock over average daily sales). Demo orders are counted and labelled.
18. Spør Søren: staff ask in plain words. The server counts the facts first (`StaffBrief`: the week's figures, open cases, failed searches, data quality, lowest stock, shipments, stock flow) and Søren only words them under the draft rules (facts only, no new numbers, cannot change or send anything, no dashes). Every answer shows the facts it used. If Ollama does not answer, the facts are shown with the error.

## Rules

1. Only persisted rows are drawn. An empty window shows an idle floor.
2. Session ID and user ID are never sent to the browser.
3. Every `/api/tarn/*` endpoint and the page check the role on the server (`ansatt`, `support`, `admin`).
4. No shop navigation, banners or chat on this page. One back arrow.
5. Weather and route conditions come from `route_conditions`, filled only by `scripts/conditions.php` (MET Norway Locationforecast and MetAlerts; Statens vegvesen's open traffic messages, which need no registration). One attempt per source and run. A source that fails is replaced by simulated rows with `is_demo = 1`, shown with a DEMO tag. The delay is labelled an estimate. Text from the sources is escaped before it is drawn.
6. Ferries come from `ferry_status`, filled by the same script from Entur: departures with Entur's realtime and cancellation flags and operator messages (Journey Planner, SIRI-ET and SIRI-SX), vessel positions (SIRI-VM). Ferries are never simulated. A crossing is "running to plan" only when live departures confirm it; timetable-only data is labelled timetable, no answer is labelled unknown. A position older than 15 minutes is drawn faded and called old. Operator messages are shown as the operator's words, because they can disagree with the departure feed.

## Acceptance

1. A user without a staff role gets a redirect to login on the page and 403 on every endpoint.
2. Station counts on the floor match the rows in the window.
3. A real search done while the panel is open appears within 5 seconds (`POLL_MS` in `tower.js`); a search without results lands in Avvik.
4. Clicking a product parcel shows its specifications with source, and Flett or Avvis works from the inspector.
5. With WebGL disabled the panel still shows counts, the log, the product list and the KPIs.
6. No horizontal scroll at phone width.
7. The transport view shows one marker per shipment with a position, and DEMO DATA while any shipment is demo.
8. Escalating from the chat without a product is rejected (400); the created ticket stores `product_id` and shows on that product in the panel.
9. KPIs include a data quality block; catalogue counts are labelled as not filtered.
10. `php scripts/transport-demo.php` refreshes the demo shipments without touching other data.
12. Buying 2 of a product lowers its online stock by 2; a demo delivery started after the purchase carries those 2 and, on arrival, raises the stock by 2. A second demo delivery carries nothing ("Tom bil") and changes no stock.
13. The map takes the mood of the worst weather on the open routes (sun bright, cloud muted, rain grey with rain, snow pale with snow, storm dark with rain and flashes; no motion with reduced motion), with a badge naming the weather, temperature range, wind and source. Each route lists its obstacles (ferry, roadwork, closure, railway crossing, traffic, warning) with severity, source and an estimated delay.
14. Innsikt shows every success goal in `docs/context/business-context.md`: besides searches, zero results, escalations and the funnel, the share of paid orders with a "goes well with" pair and the median minutes from a session's first search to cart, each for the week against the week before. A goal without data says so instead of showing 0.
15. The transport map draws each ferry crossing near the routes coloured by status (green live and on time, amber delayed or a disruption message, red a live cancellation, grey timetable only or unknown) with the vessels where Entur last placed them. A shipment that crosses shows the crossing's status, the operator message, the next departures each tagged live, timetable or cancelled, how many vessels report a position and how old the newest is, the source and when it was checked.
11. On a search without results: mark as reviewed shows the reviewer and time; mapping the term to a product makes the next search for it return that product (support gets 403); a task without a title is rejected (400), a task with one shows under Oppgaver until closed.
